#### Abstract
The realization of ferroelectricity in wurtzite AlN-based heterostructural alloys has opened the possibility of directly integrating ferroelectrics with conventional microelectronics based on tetrahedral semiconductors (Si, SiC and III-Vs), enabling compute-in-memory architectures, high-density data storage, and more. However, wurtzite ferroelectrics generally require enormous electric fields, approaching breakdown, to reverse their polarization. The discovery of wurtzite ferroelectrics has been driven to date by chemical intuition and empirical explorations. In this talk, I will share our work on computation-guided design of wurtzite ferroelectrics with low coercive fields and focus on three key aspects: (1) Discovery of novel wurtzite single-phase compounds and alloys that exhibit ferroelectricity, (2) Insights into the atomic- and domain-scale polarization switching mechanisms, and (3) Effect of native defects and unintentional impurities on the ferroelectric behavior. Our computational predictions have led to the experimental realization of new wurtzite ferroelectrics and guided the choice of synthesis conditions that optimize the dielectric and ferroelectric properties.

#### Biography
Dr. Prashun Gorai is an Assistant Professor in Chemical & Biological Engineering at RPI. He received his bachelor’s degree from IIT Madras (India) and his PhD from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. He was a postdoctoral fellow and a Research Assistant Professor at the Colorado School of Mines and the National Laboratory of the Rockies (formerly, NREL). His team uses quantum-mechanical calculations, high-throughput computing, and machine learning to discover and design functional materials for energy and next-generation microelectronics. He closely collaborates with experimentalists in the US and internationally. The International Thermoelectric Society awarded him the Young Investigator Award in 2022. _Materials Horizons_ (Royal Society of Chemistry) and _ACS Materials Letters_ (American Chemical Society) have recognized him as an Emerging Investigator. He is a recipient of the _Chemistry of Materials_ Lectureship and Best Paper Award 2023.
