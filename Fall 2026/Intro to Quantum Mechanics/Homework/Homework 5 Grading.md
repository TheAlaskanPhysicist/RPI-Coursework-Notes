

## 4.1.A
Show the following
$$\begin{align}
+\dfrac{\hbar}{2} && \left|_x\left<+\middle|\psi(t=0)\right>\right|^2&=\dfrac{1}{2}\\
-\dfrac{\hbar}{2} && \left|_x\left<-\middle|\psi(t=0)\right>\right|^2&=\dfrac{1}{2}\\
\end{align}$$
## 4.1.B

$$\begin{align}
\ket{\psi(t)}&=\cos\left(\dfrac{\omega_0t}{2}\right)\ket{+}+\sin\left(\dfrac{\omega_0t}{2}\right)\ket{-} & \omega_0&=-\gamma B_0

## 4.1.C
Show the following
$$\begin{align}
S_x:+\dfrac{\hbar}{2} && \left|_x\left<+\middle|\psi(t=0)\right>\right|^2&=\dfrac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\sin\omega_0 t\\
S_x:-\dfrac{\hbar}{2} && \left|_x\left<-\middle|\psi(t=0)\right>\right|^2&=\dfrac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{2}\sin\omega_0 t\\
S_y:+\dfrac{\hbar}{2} && \left|_x\left<+\middle|\psi(t=0)\right>\right|^2&=\dfrac{1}{2}\\
S_y:-\dfrac{\hbar}{2} && \left|_x\left<-\middle|\psi(t=0)\right>\right|^2&=\dfrac{1}{2}\\
S_z:+\dfrac{\hbar}{2} && \left|_x\left<+\middle|\psi(t=0)\right>\right|^2&=\dfrac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\cos\omega_0 t\\
S_z:-\dfrac{\hbar}{2} && \left|_x\left<-\middle|\psi(t=0)\right>\right|^2&=\dfrac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{2}\cos\omega_0 t\\
\end{align}$$


## 4.4.A
Show the following
$$\begin{align}
\ket{\psi_1}=\ket{\psi(T)}&=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left[\left(\cos\frac{\theta}{2}+\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\right)e^{-i\omega_0T/2}\ket{+}_x+\left(\cos\frac{\theta}{2}-\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\right)e^{+i\omega_0T/2}\ket{-}_x\right]
\end{align}$$
## 4.4.B
Show the following
$$\begin{align}
\left|\left<+\middle|\psi_1\right>\right|^2&=\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2} & \left|\left<-\middle|\psi_1\right>\right|^2&=\sin^2\frac{\theta}{2}
\end{align}$$
## 4.4.C
Show the following
$$\begin{align}
\left\langle S_x\right\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_1S_x)&=\dfrac{\hbar}{2}\sin\theta\\
\left\langle S_y\right\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_1S_y)&=-\dfrac{\hbar}{2}\cos\theta\sin\omega_0T\\
\left\langle S_z\right\rangle=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_1S_z)&=\dfrac{\hbar}{2}\cos\theta\cos\omega_0T
\end{align}$$
The operator $\rho_1$ describes a pure state, and $\rho_1^2=\rho_1$ (projection operator).
