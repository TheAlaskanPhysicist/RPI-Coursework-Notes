



State
$$\begin{align}
\ket{\psi}&=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\circlearrowleft}\ket{\circlearrowright}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\circlearrowright}\ket{\circlearrowleft}
\end{align}$$
Dual Rail Encoding
$$\begin{align}
\ket{\psi}&=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{1}\ket{0}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{0}\ket{1}
\end{align}$$



Let $\ket{0}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\circlearrowleft}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\circlearrowright}$ and $\ket{1}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\circlearrowleft}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{\circlearrowright}$, and so substituting in we get:
$$\begin{align}
\ket{\psi}&=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{1}\ket{0}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ket{0}\ket{1}\\
&=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}^3}\left(\ket{\circlearrowleft}-\ket{\circlearrowright}\right)\left(\ket{\circlearrowleft}+\ket{\circlearrowright}\right)+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}^3}\left(\ket{\circlearrowleft}+\ket{\circlearrowright}\right)\left(\ket{\circlearrowleft}-\ket{\circlearrowright}\right)\\
&=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}^3}\left(\ket{\circlearrowleft}\ket{\circlearrowleft}-\ket{\circlearrowright}\ket{\circlearrowleft}+\ket{\circlearrowleft}\ket{\circlearrowright}-\ket{\circlearrowright}\ket{\circlearrowright}\right)+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}^3}\left(\ket{\circlearrowleft}\ket{\circlearrowleft}-\ket{\circlearrowleft}\ket{\circlearrowright}+\ket{\circlearrowright}\ket{\circlearrowleft}-\ket{\circlearrowright}\ket{\circlearrowright}\right)\\
&=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}^3}\left(2\ket{\circlearrowleft}\ket{\circlearrowleft}-2\ket{\circlearrowright}\ket{\circlearrowright}\right)\\
\ket{\psi}&=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\ket{\circlearrowleft}\ket{\circlearrowleft}-\ket{\circlearrowright}\ket{\circlearrowright}\right)
\end{align}$$
