**Born Reciprocity**
Max Born in 1949 proposed physics should be symmetrically invariant under the exchange of spacetime coordinates and momentum coordinates $(x^\mu\leftrightarrow p_\mu)$. He hypothesized that just as special relativity bounds velocity via a spacetime metric, a reciprocal theory should bound maximum acceleration and force.

Explorations into the geometry of momentum space for quantum gravity and non-commutative geometry has been explored by Giovanni Amelino-Camelia, Laurent Freidel, and Jerzy Kowalski-Glikman.

Kähler and Symplectic Field Theories using $z^\mu=x^\mu+ik^\mu$ directly parallel geometric quantization, coherent states ,and complexified phase-spaced manifolds.
